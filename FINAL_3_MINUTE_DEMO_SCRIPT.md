### **Tone & Pace**:
- **Enthusiastic but conversational** (not salesy)
- **Speak 10% slower** than normal conversation
- **Pause after key points** (let them sink in)
- **Smile while talking** (you can hear it in voice!)
- **Energy level: 7/10** (excited, not manic)

### **Visual Guidelines**:

**What to Show**:
✅ Your face (builds trust) for hook and CTA  
✅ Live demo (proof it works)  
✅ Progress indicators (transparency)  
✅ Results (94.3% accuracy, etc.)

**What NOT to Show**:

❌ Long loading screens (cut them out)  
❌ Errors or bugs  
❌ Complex technical details

- 94.3% Accuracy

- $25/mo (vs $5,000/mo)
