## 🎥 Recording Steps

### Step 1: Prepare Your Screen (5 min)
```
1. Open DataFlowHub at localhost:5173 or dataflowhub.ai
2. Sign in to your account
3. Go to Data Management page
4. Have a sample CSV file ready (sales data works great)
5. Close all other tabs and notifications
```

### Step 2: Set Up OBS/Loom (3 min)
```
OBS Settings:
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- FPS: 30
- Audio: Your microphone

Loom:
- Just click "Screen + Cam" or "Screen Only"
```

### Step 3: Record Each Scene Separately
```
Scene 1: Record spreadsheet + voice hook (5 sec)
Scene 2: Record upload action (15 sec)
Scene 3: Record data profiler (15 sec)  
Scene 4: Record AI chat (15 sec)
Scene 5: Record landing page + CTA (10 sec)
```

### Step 4: Edit in CapCut (10 min)
```
1. Import all clips
2. Arrange in order
3. Add auto-captions (essential!)
4. Add background music (YouTube Audio Library)
5. Export at 1080p
```

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Record multiple takes** - Pick the best one
2. **Speak slowly** - AI captions work better
3. **Show your cursor** - Helps viewers follow
4. **Use keyboard shortcuts** - Looks more professional
5. **Add zoom effects** - CapCut can zoom on important parts
6. **Publish Tuesday-Thursday** - Best engagement days
7. **Reply to ALL comments** - Boosts algorithm
