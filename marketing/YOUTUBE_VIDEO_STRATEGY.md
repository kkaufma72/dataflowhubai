## 🎤 Recording Tips

1. **Use screen recording**: OBS (free) or Loom
2. **Add voiceover**: Record separately for cleaner audio
3. **Face cam optional**: But increases trust
4. **Captions required**: 85% watch without sound
5. **Background music**: Epidemic Sound or YouTube Audio Library
